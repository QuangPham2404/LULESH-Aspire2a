## MPI scaling

All runs used the existing CCE13 Release/O3 binary, `-s 30`, and one OpenMP
thread per rank.

| MPI ranks | PBS job | Runtime | FOM | Correctness |
| ---: | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 1 | `15097040.pbs101` | 20 s | 1258.0879 | passed |
| 8 | `15097041.pbs101` | 70 s | 6230.7687 | passed |
| 27 | `15097042.pbs101` | 170 s | 13372.882 | passed |

Because LULESH interprets `-s 30` per MPI domain, total work increases with
MPI rank count. These results are weak-scaling-style measurements rather than
fixed-global-size strong-scaling measurements.

The first MPI submission failed due to a relative shared-runner path. The
failure was recorded, the runner was changed to an absolute project-root path,
and the corrected retries succeeded.
